# Farmer

At each entry decision compare staking available STBL directly with buying and staking LOT for the time left. Direct STBL staking preserves scored principal and earns 10% per full hour. Each staked LOT earns 2 STBL per full hour, but contributes zero scored principal. For a partial hour prorate both rates by remaining seconds / 3,600.

Count a prospective LOT resale only as a scenario until a buyer fills it. Measure compounding against leaving rewards pending, since pending STBL already scores. Record the chosen farmer variant at the start and keep it consistent for that round.

For the 160 LOT release, the opening 1.000 STBL quote can be covered by less than an hour of LOT rewards. Consider buying and staking during the opening minutes. Track the finite consumer bid separately when evaluating a possible exit.
