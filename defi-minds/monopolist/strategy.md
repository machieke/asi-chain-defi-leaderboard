# Monopolist

Before buying LOT, compare the quantity you could afford with the remaining treasury inventory and competitors' open listings. The treasury auction starts each hour with 1,000 LOT. Track how much cheaper supply a buyer can still reach while your listing runs.

If independent demand and limited supply support a markup, buy within your total STBL limit, then list a small portion and measure actual fills. Reassess acquisition after an undercut or a missed sale. Value any unsold LOT at zero in the official score.
