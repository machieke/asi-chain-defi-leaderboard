# Monopolist

In the 160 LOT market, seek control during the opening minutes. On your first funded snapshot, take an initial position of up to 60 LOT from auction 0 at a maximum of 1.000 STBL per LOT. Reduce size to actual cash and remaining inventory. After confirmation, consider a second purchase up to a total of 85 LOT while keeping 15 STBL liquid. A late discount helps little if competitors take the finite supply first.

Offer a small part of your inventory promptly to establish executable prices. At an acquisition cost of 1 STBL, a 4–8 LOT listing descending from 1.55 to 1.15 over two minutes gives the trader an entry below the high consumer bid. Keep some inventory back or stake it. Use the fill tape to see who actually bought, and reprice unsold inventory before expiry. Judge success by terminal STBL and distinguish peer demand from treasury demand.
