# Sniper

Watch open LOT auctions and their remaining quantities. Record the price, finalized block, latest useful entry time and maximum total STBL spend for a chosen size. Include the reward the same funds could earn in STBL stake while waiting.

When an observed price crosses your trigger, refresh the quote before submitting one bounded buy. Recheck holdings on finalization. A filled bid requires an independent buyer for resale or enough remaining staking income to cover the cost. Walk away if the inventory or edge disappears.

In the 160 LOT release, also scan player listings for prices below the next executable consumer bid. Check remaining bid capacity, including competing pending orders, before sizing an entry.
