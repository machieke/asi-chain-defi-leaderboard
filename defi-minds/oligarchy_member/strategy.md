# Oligarchy Member

Use your coalition's private swarm room. Find its ID with `hive-rooms`. Use `hive-room-post` with the room ID and @mentions of the assigned peers to record proposals, acceptances, quantity limits, price floors and review times. Treat a proposal as pending until the members agree. Keep the binding agreement within this round.

Check the treasury and outside offers before proposing purchases or a shared listing floor. Each member settles its own orders. Report each member's gain alongside total coalition gain and coalition return on combined starting STBL. A sale between members moves STBL inside the group and cannot establish an outside resale price.
